Elastic string: pull and release

One end stays fixed. Your hand holds the other until you let go.

Move the orange end up and down to send a wave. Release it to let the 4 m string fall, bend and swing.

Held — drag the orange end or press Let go.

Mouse or touch. Keyboard: focus the scene, Space grabs/releases, arrows move the held end, Escape releases, R resets.

Gravity, elastic tension, bending rigidity, extension damping and bending damping, plus gentle artificial settling damping at 0.8/s. No air resistance, ground or self-contact. The hand moves at up to 6 m/s of physical time and reaches at most 4.6 m from the fixed end. Release starts the endpoint from rest; the rest of the string keeps moving. Read the model.
