Starting the pressure solve…

The flow obeys a coarse-grid, two-dimensional incompressible viscous projection model. Its viscosity is deliberately much higher than air's. The blocked cells approximate a symmetric wing section; there is no spanwise flow, tip vortex, calibrated lift, or credible real-wing stall prediction. Trails are pathlines, not prescribed curves or buoyant smoke. Read the experiment and equations.
