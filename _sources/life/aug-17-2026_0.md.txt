# Aug 17, 2026

Yep, lowering the mainsail killed the boat speed while heaving to.

More things I learned:

- Tie five butterfly knots every foot or so to make a short rope ladder.
- Hang it from a whinch or cleat to climb aboard from water.
- Let a long end drift behind the boat.
- It's okay to go for a swim while heaving to if you can climb back.
- Even when single-handing.
- If you stare at windex long enough, you learn how the wind feels on your face.
- Then you can sail without looking at windex.
- If you listen to the sails long enough, you don't need to look at them.
- It's okay to sail at night if you know the area, can go without windex, and know how sails sound.
- Garmin Navionics is helpful too.
- When keeping bearing using compass, turn tiller towards the azimuth you want to be on.
- When running turn tiller towards the sail that luffs.
- Foilers love to catch motorboat wakes. Some people run the boats just to give them a ride.
- Wakes kill the sailboat speed though.
- Don't try to tack over a wake.
